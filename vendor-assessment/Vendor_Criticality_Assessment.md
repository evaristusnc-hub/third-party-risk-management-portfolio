# Vendor Criticality Assessment

## Vendor Overview

**Vendor:** CloudServe Technologies  
**Service:** SaaS Customer Management Platform  
**Business Owner:** Sales Operations  
**Assessment Type:** Pre-Onboarding Third-Party Risk Assessment

## Criticality Assessment

| Risk Factor | Assessment | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| Sensitive Data Access | Processes customer PII | High |
| System Access | Integrates with internal systems | High |
| Business Dependency | Supports critical customer operations | High |
| Regulatory Impact | Privacy and security requirements apply | High |
| Service Availability | Service disruption could affect operations | High |

## Overall Criticality

**Criticality Rating: High**

The vendor is classified as a high-criticality third party because it processes sensitive customer information, integrates with internal systems, and supports important business operations. Enhanced security due diligence is therefore required before onboarding.

# Risk Scoring Methodology

This methodology is used to evaluate third-party risks consistently based on likelihood and impact.

## Likelihood Scale

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 - Rare | Unlikely to occur |
| 2 - Unlikely | Could occur, but not expected |
| 3 - Possible | May occur under certain conditions |
| 4 - Likely | Expected to occur in many circumstances |
| 5 - Almost Certain | Expected to occur frequently |

## Impact Scale

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 - Insignificant | Minimal business or security impact |
| 2 - Minor | Limited operational or compliance impact |
| 3 - Moderate | Noticeable business, security, or compliance impact |
| 4 - Major | Significant operational, financial, regulatory, or reputational impact |
| 5 - Severe | Critical business disruption, major data exposure, or serious regulatory impact |

## Risk Calculation

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

## Risk Rating Thresholds

| Risk Score | Rating |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

## Risk Assessment Principle

Risk ratings should reflect both the probability of the risk occurring and the potential business impact if it occurs. Ratings should be supported by available evidence and documented rationale.

## Treatment Summary

CloudServe Technologies should establish documented vulnerability remediation timelines based on vulnerability severity. The remediation process should include defined service-level expectations, escalation procedures, exception management, and evidence of remediation.

The risk will remain open until the required remediation measures are implemented and validated.

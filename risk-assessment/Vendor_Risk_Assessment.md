# Vendor Risk Assessment

## Vendor

**Vendor:** CloudServe Technologies  
**Service:** SaaS Customer Management Platform  
**Vendor Criticality:** High

## Identified Control Gap

The vendor does not currently maintain defined remediation timelines for critical vulnerabilities.

## Risk Statement

If critical vulnerabilities are not remediated within defined timelines, exploitable weaknesses may remain exposed for extended periods, increasing the likelihood of unauthorized access, data compromise, service disruption, and regulatory or reputational impact.

## Risk Evaluation

| Risk Factor | Rating | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Likelihood | 4 - Likely | Unresolved critical vulnerabilities may remain exploitable until remediation occurs |
| Impact | 4 - Major | Exploitation could affect sensitive customer data, integrated systems, and critical business operations |

## Risk Score

**Likelihood × Impact = Risk Score**

**4 × 4 = 16**

## Overall Risk Rating

**Risk Rating: High**

A score of 16 falls within the High risk range defined in the Risk Scoring Methodology.

## Recommended Action

The vendor should establish documented vulnerability remediation timelines based on severity, including defined timelines for critical and high-risk vulnerabilities. Exceptions should require documented approval and compensating controls where appropriate.

The identified risk should be tracked through remediation and reassessed before final risk acceptance or onboarding approval.
